University of Mississippi · a guide to the badges you'll see on assignments — what's allowed, what isn't, and what counts as acceptable AI use.

Your instructor marks each assignment with a few of these badges. The colored circle in the corner tells you the answer at a glance; the wording underneath tells you exactly what it means for this assignment. When a badge and the written instructions disagree, follow the written instructions and ask your instructor.
